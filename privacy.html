Privacy Policy

Ayah – Quran app for Android · Effective 25 November 2026

Ayah is made by Vaibhav Ghanghav ("we"). This policy explains what information the app uses and why. In short: there is no sign-up, your reading data and location stay on your phone, and the only third parties involved are Google (ads and Play purchases) and RevenueCat (purchases).

1. No account

You do not need to create an account or give us your name, email address or phone number to use Ayah.

2. Information that stays on your phone

The following is stored only on your device and is never sent to us:

  • Saved ayahs, notes, reading progress and streaks
  • Your settings (language, theme, translation, notification times, prayer calculation method)
  • Recitation audio you choose to download

Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data in Android settings, deletes all of it.

3. Location

If you allow it, Ayah uses your approximate location to calculate prayer times and the Qibla direction. This calculation happens on your phone. Your location is not sent to us, not sent to advertisers, and not included in any analytics. You can refuse location permission and choose your city by hand instead, and you can turn the permission off at any time in Android settings.

4. Notifications and alarms

Daily ayah reminders and prayer alerts are scheduled on your phone. No server is involved.

5. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version of Ayah shows ads from Google AdMob in a few places. Ads are never shown on the Quran text, the Mushaf reader, the audio player, Qibla or prayer alerts, and never close to prayer times. To show ads, Google's SDK may collect:

  • your device's advertising ID,
  • IP address, device model, operating system and app version,
  • information about the ads shown and whether you tapped them.

Google uses this to deliver ads, measure them and prevent fraud. If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, Ayah asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown, and you can change your choice later. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads). Ads in Ayah are limited to content rated suitable for general audiences.

See how Google uses information from apps that use its services and Google's Privacy Policy.

6. Purchases (Google Play and RevenueCat)

Ayah Premium is a one-time purchase made through Google Play. Payment is handled entirely by Google; we never see your card or bank details. We use RevenueCat to confirm purchases and restore them on a new phone. RevenueCat receives a random app user ID and your purchase record (product, date, price, Google Play order ID). See RevenueCat's Privacy Policy.

7. Downloads

When you download or stream recitation audio, your phone requests the files from the server that hosts them. Like any web server, it may record your IP address and the time of the request for security and to keep the service running. These records are not used to identify you.

8. Usage analytics

Ayah currently sends no usage analytics. If we add anonymous usage statistics in the future, they will be off unless you turn them on in Settings, will never include your location, notes or any personal details, and this policy will be updated first.

9. Sharing

We do not sell your information. Apart from what is described in sections 5 and 6, nothing is shared with anyone. When you share an ayah image, it goes only to the app you choose.

10. Children

Ayah is for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

11. Security

Information stored on your phone is protected by Android's app sandbox. Connections to Google and RevenueCat are encrypted (HTTPS).

12. Your choices

  • Turn location permission off and pick your city by hand.
  • Change ad consent (EEA/UK) and reset your advertising ID.
  • Buy Premium to remove ads.
  • Delete all app data by clearing it in Android settings or uninstalling.

Because we do not hold personal data about you on our servers, there is usually nothing for us to delete. To ask about data held by Google or RevenueCat, use the links above or contact us.

13. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top. Significant changes will also be mentioned in the app's release notes.
